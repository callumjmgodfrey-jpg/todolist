How it works

  • Click Allow Notifications to grant browser permission.
  • Toggle the switch on to arm your daily reminder.
  • Use Test Notification to preview the notification immediately.
  • The scheduled reminder fires each day near your chosen time — the page must be open (or a service worker is needed for true background scheduling, not included here).
  • Your permission status and preference are saved locally.
